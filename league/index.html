Questions from venues

Do we need special equipment?

No. A dartboard and a playing area are enough. Scoring runs on phones or a board-side tablet.

Your events appear in the app's local events feed and on this website.

Can we run leagues as well as tournaments?

Yes. Run one-off tournaments, weekly leagues and Premier League series.
